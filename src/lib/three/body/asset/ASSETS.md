# Body asset provenance

`mh-body.ts` is generated by `scripts/build-body-asset.mjs` from the MakeHuman
assets (base mesh `3dobjs/base.obj`, morph targets under `targets/`, skeleton
`rigs/default.mhskel` and skin weights `rigs/default_weights.mhw`), downloaded from
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/makehuman/data/

Those assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md);
no attribution is required. Credit: the MakeHuman community (Data Collection AB,
Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier and contributors).

The builder applies athletic-male morph targets, fits the mesh to our rig's bind
skeleton, maps the weights to our bones and transfers the muscle map from the
procedural anatomy (src/lib/three/body/anatomy.ts). Re-run it after changing the
skeleton or the anatomy: `node scripts/build-body-asset.mjs`.
